# Current publication results (WiNN 0.1.6, 11 November 2026)

These tables hold the results reported in the manuscript. All WiNN rows come from the WiNN 0.1.6 re-run; Raw and comparator rows (ComBat, QC-RLSC, QC-RFSC, TIGER and SERRF) are reused unchanged from the validated previous release, spliced by `analysis/splice_winn_rerun.R`. Empirical QC-RFSC point endpoints reproduce the corrected-adapter rows of Supplementary Table S4; they have no revised paired uncertainty, and no previous-adapter uncertainty is reused.
